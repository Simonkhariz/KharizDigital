# Portfolio inventory

Inventory of the two source folders that will feed the Khariz Digital portfolio. Source material was only read; nothing in either folder, `site/demos/` or `site/` was changed. The machine-readable version is `portfolio-inventory.json` in this folder.

## 1. Summary

- **Folder A** (`source-material/website design pictures/`): 51 files, 50 JPEG and 1 PNG. 47 are unique by checksum; 4 are byte-identical copies of another file. One file (IMG_3112.PNG) is a portrait photo, not a design.
- The 46 unique design images form **32 project groups**: 6 groups with more than one image, 26 single-image groups.
- **Batch 1** (every group containing a `pro` image): 9 projects. All nine `pro` files belong to nine different projects.
- Almost everything in Folder A is the top of a SaaS-style landing page. Only 3 `pro` projects have enough reference to rebuild without inventing sections (Converto and Ninny 2.0 show a full page; Axion shows about two thirds).
- IMG_3327 to IMG_3332 were expected to be sections of one page. They are **six different brands** captured in the same format.
- IMG_3246 to IMG_3248 are a three-part capture of one page (Sigil); this run was not in the original list of suspected runs.
- **Folder B** (`source-material/Webild templates__ 2/`): one compiled bundle, 23 page templates, all 23 present as readable source in `site/demos/`.
- Every screenshot shows a brand name and many show real third-party logos. Each project below has a proposed fictional name; the names have not been checked against trademarks or existing companies.
